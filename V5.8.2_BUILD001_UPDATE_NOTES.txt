Manufacturer Roll Number Field
Date: 2026-07-31

- Adds Manufacturer Roll # to New Roll Work / Carpet Receiving.
- Supports manufacturer and mill roll numbers containing letters, numbers, and hyphens.
- Automatically trims spaces, removes unsupported characters, converts letters to uppercase, and limits the value to 20 characters.
- Keeps Operational Roll # and Manufacturer Roll # as separate fields.
- Saves Manufacturer Roll # into Carpet Inventory instead of hiding it in Notes.
- Shows the field in Carpet Roll editing, roll details, labels, search, CSV export, cutting workflow, and operation drafts/history.
- Prevents duplicate Manufacturer Roll # values when creating or editing carpet rolls.
- Existing numeric values such as 5254 remain fully supported, along with values such as SHW-8K27.
