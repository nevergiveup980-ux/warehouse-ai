Scope

2. Zero-quantity records remain stored and available for audit/history; no inventory data is deleted.
3. Records marked Quantity Pending remain visible as COUNT/PENDING even when their temporary numeric quantity is zero.
4. The shared rule applies consistently to product cards, Manage Product Inventory, the Inventory list, current totals, dashboard counts, the warehouse map and low-stock views.
5. Reserved, transferred, shipped and archived lifecycle behavior remains unchanged.

Validation expectation
- Positive ACTIVE GENERAL record: visible and counted.
- Zero ACTIVE GENERAL record: hidden and not counted.
- Zero Quantity Pending record: visible for physical verification.
- Archived/history record: preserved and excluded from current inventory as before.
