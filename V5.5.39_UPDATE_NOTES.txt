RUNLU Warehouse AI V5.5.39

Inventory Transfer Deduction Fix

- Fixed a transfer bug where the destination update reused the source inventoryRecordId.
- Previously, the source record was deducted and then the same record was immediately credited back, leaving the visible balance unchanged.
- The destination leg now clears inventoryRecordId and finds or creates a separate inventory record at the destination location.
- Example: 92 Box transferred by 57 Box now leaves source balance 35 Box and creates/adds 57 Box at the destination.
