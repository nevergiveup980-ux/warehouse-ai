RUNLU Warehouse AI V6.12.33 Build128

Operation Save Authority

Observed on iPhone: ordinary Work controls remained responsive, but the green Complete Work / Complete & Apply Stock button could silently do nothing while other buttons still worked.

Root cause:
A legacy Carpet Receiving identity guard owns a global checking/busy lane. Non-carpet Work saves still passed through that carpet-only lane. If the guard remained busy after a prior carpet identity check, saveOperation returned false without feedback even though Cancel, Delete Draft and other controls continued to work.

Build128:
- Routes Carpet Receiving through the approved Build125/126/127 safety path unchanged.
- Routes all other Work saves directly to the pre-Build120 save chain.
- Prevents Build120 / Build127 from re-wrapping or replacing the new save authority.
- Converts unexpected save exceptions into a visible Work save stopped message instead of a silent no-op.
- Does not relax exact inventory requirements for Stock work.
