PEOPLE TO CALL · CORRECTED BUSINESS MEANING
- People to Call is a Sales follow-up folder, not a supplier-chasing / warehouse exception folder.
- An order enters People to Call only after pickup / receiving is complete (Ready or Completed).
- Ready / Completed automatically creates the Sales follow-up flag unless that follow-up was already completed.
- Sales can mark the follow-up done; the order then leaves People to Call.
- Sales can reopen follow-up on a Ready / Completed order if another call is needed.

WHAT NO LONGER ENTERS PEOPLE TO CALL
- Supplier confirmation waiting.
- Delayed pickup.
- Overdue pickup date.
- Pickup due today / tomorrow.
These remain operational status / alert conditions and are handled by the existing Warehouse receiving workflow.

FOUR PHYSICAL FOLDERS
1. Awaiting Confirmation — PO sent, supplier confirmation pending.
2. Pick-ups Next Month — confirmed pickup in next calendar month.
3. Pick-ups This Month — confirmed pickup in current calendar month.
4. People to Call — pickup / receiving finished; Sales follow-up pending.

DATA SAFETY
- Existing receiving quantities, Stock Inventory posting, Accounting flow, Flooring PO handoff and Build109 carpet labels are unchanged.
- Training-only Supabase functions were corrected to match the real business workflow.
