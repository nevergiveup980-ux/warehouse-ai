RUNLU Warehouse AI V6.12.22 · Build117
Date: 2026-09-10

COMMAND CENTER CLOUD HISTORY RECOVERY

What changed
- Daily Work History now reads the cloud Operations dataset directly instead of relying only on this browser's local cache.
- Cloud records and local/offline records are merged by stable operation identity and de-duplicated.
- When the same operation exists in both places, the newer record wins.
- History refreshes on page entry, focus, visibility return and every 15 seconds while Command Center history is open.
- A small History Cloud status and Refresh History control were added below the daily history count.

Safety
- Build117 is display/history synchronization only.
- It does not post inventory, alter carpet balances, cut rolls, ship material, transfer stock, receive product or replay historical inventory impact.
- Existing local/offline history remains available when cloud access is unavailable.

Regression target
- A browser whose local history stops at Sep 4 must still display cloud work dated Sep 8, Sep 9 and Sep 10.
