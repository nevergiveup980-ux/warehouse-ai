RUNLU Warehouse AI V6.3.1 Build032
TTS Latency Polish + Smarter Lane Handoff

Changes
- Keeps Build031 Dual Controller Architecture intact.
- Shows the warehouse answer text immediately before TTS begins.
- Adds a 1.5 second TTS startup watchdog on iOS; if speech does not actually start, the app falls back to the visible text and resumes continuous listening instead of appearing stuck.
- Shortens post-answer microphone handoff delay for a more natural conversation rhythm.
- Successful Chinese recognition keeps the next listening lane in Chinese; successful English recognition keeps the next lane in English.
- Lane handoff is based on the detected user speech, not on the language used by the spoken reply.
- Existing low-confidence bilingual fallback remains available when the next turn switches languages.

No warehouse inventory, cloud sync, permissions, or business workflow schema changes.
