RUNLU Warehouse AI V5.5.35 — Carpet & Installer Transfer Upgrade

1. Inventory Transfer is now a clearly named operation.
2. Added transfer routes:
   - Warehouse → Store
   - Store → Warehouse
   - Warehouse → Installer
   - Installer → Warehouse
   - Warehouse → Warehouse
3. Added a dedicated active Carpet Roll selector.
4. Selecting a carpet roll automatically fills product, colour, current location and roll details.
5. Supports partial-length transfer or whole-roll transfer.
6. Partial carpet transfer creates a traceable child roll at the destination and reduces the source roll once.
7. Whole-roll transfer changes only the roll location.
8. General products such as Spill Blocker can still use Existing Inventory Record and quantity/unit transfer.
