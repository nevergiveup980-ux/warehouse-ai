RUNLU V5.7.4 Build001 — Inventory Lifecycle Foundation
Date: 2026-07-30

CHANGES
- Adds lifecycle metadata to every general inventory record:
  inventoryType, lifecycleStatus, sourceModule, sourceId, createdAt, lastUpdatedAt.
- Migrates older inventory records safely on startup.
- Current Inventory now counts only ACTIVE + GENERAL records.
- Customer-order reserved, transferred, shipped and archived records remain outside current totals.
- Replaces normal Delete Record buttons with Archive Record.
- Archiving removes a record from current inventory while retaining audit/history data.
- New Receiving records are explicitly created as ACTIVE GENERAL inventory and retain their receiving source.
- Completed customer-order stock is marked RESERVED + ARCHIVED with its PO as source ID.

IMPORTANT
Permanent deletion remains blocked in normal warehouse workflow. Use Archive Record for completed, shipped, duplicate or no-longer-current inventory records.
