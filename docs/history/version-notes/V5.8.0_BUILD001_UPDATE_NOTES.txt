RUNLU Warehouse OS V5.8.0 Build001

PARTIAL ORDER MANAGEMENT
- Line items now support Pending, Picked Up, Received, Backorder and Completed.
- Mixed received/open line items automatically set the overall PO to Partial.
- Received/Completed lines can update inventory immediately while Backorder/Pending lines remain open.
- Reopening the PO later allows remaining line items to be received without reapplying completed lines.

SUPPLIER RECEIPT FORMAT LIBRARY
- Added built-in recognition templates for Primco, Taiga, Fuzion, Treeco, Buckwold, Centura, Oakel City and Twelve Oaks.
- Expanded units to CTN, CT, EA, PAIL, SF and SQFT.
- Supplier scan remains review-first and never changes inventory automatically.
