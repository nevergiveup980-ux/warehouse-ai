RUNLU Warehouse AI V5.5.43

1. Warehouse Map colours standardized:
   - Green: available capacity / product area
   - Yellow: nearly full
   - Red: full, over capacity, or unavailable
   - Gray: empty, unused, or non-inventory area

2. Customer Order lifecycle added:
   - Draft
   - In Progress
   - Completed
   - Archived
   - Only Draft orders can be deleted.
   - Completed orders record completion time and completed-by, lock editing, remain searchable, and may be archived.
   - Completed orders older than 180 days are automatically archived when the order list is opened.
   - Complete Order and Archive actions added.

3. Existing V5.5.42 receiving/product-link fix retained.
