RUNLU Warehouse OS V6.12.17 · Build110
Date: 2026-08-31

SUPPLIER PICKUP · PHYSICAL-FOLDER WORKFLOW
- Added four live work queues that mirror the warehouse's real paper folders:
  1. Awaiting Confirmation
  2. Pick-ups Next Month
  3. Pick-ups This Month
  4. People to Call
- The queues sit inside the existing Supplier Pickup / Receiving module instead of creating a separate parallel workflow.

SUPPLIER CONFIRMATION
- Added a Supplier Confirmed action to each open PO task.
- Confirmation can store or update the confirmed pickup date plus an optional supplier note.
- Existing tasks that had already moved into warehouse execution were backfilled as confirmed when appropriate.

AUTOMATIC MONTH ROLLOVER
- Pick-ups This Month and Pick-ups Next Month are calculated from the current calendar month and the confirmed pickup date.
- No manual month-to-month filing step is required.
- When the calendar changes, confirmed tasks move between the month queues automatically.

PEOPLE TO CALL
- Added manual Add to People to Call and Call Resolved actions.
- The queue also surfaces automatic follow-up triggers for:
  · supplier confirmation waiting 2+ days;
  · delayed tasks;
  · overdue pickup dates;
  · pickup due today or tomorrow while still Scheduled.

DATA / SAFETY
- Added Supplier confirmation and call-follow-up fields to flooring_supplier_tasks in the existing training environment.
- Added dedicated training-only RPCs for confirmation and call-follow-up updates.
- Existing receiving, stock posting, accounting, PO handoff, inventory, and Build109 carpet-label behavior remain unchanged.
