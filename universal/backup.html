Backup & Restore

Back
Your warehouse. Your backup.
The backup file is encrypted on this device before you save or share it. RUNLU does not receive a copy.

Create encrypted backup

Owner only. The backup includes this warehouse workspace, operational records and local user accounts. Choose a backup password that is different from the warehouse PIN.
RUNLU cannot recover a forgotten backup password. Keep it somewhere safe.

Restore encrypted backup

Use this on a new device or after reinstalling. Restore replaces the RUNLU Warehouse OS workspace currently stored on this device. After restore, sign in with a user and PIN from the backup.
