Build045 changes
----------------
1. Admin Recovery
   - Authorized RUNLU administrator account can restore Administrator on a trusted device.
   - Unauthorized Warehouse Staff cannot self-promote.
   - Authorized owner sign-in restores Administrator automatically.

2. Voice Intent Priority
   - Every new utterance re-evaluates quantity/location intent.
   - Chinese quantity phrases such as 有多少 / 还有多少 / 几卷 are explicit quantity intent.

3. Context Confidence Guard
   - Short unclear recognition such as an unknown one-word fragment will not silently reuse old context.
   - Voice asks for confirmation instead.

4. Inventory Certainty Awareness
   - COUNT PENDING / PHYSICAL COUNT REQUIRED is never treated as zero.
   - Confirmed quantity and pending-count records are reported separately.
   - Generic Heather Choice queries aggregate confirmed variants plus pending physical-count records.
