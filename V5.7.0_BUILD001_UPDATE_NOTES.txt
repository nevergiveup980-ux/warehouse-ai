RUNLU Warehouse AI V5.7.0 — Draft & Resume Build001

NEW
- Added a real Save Draft action to Customer Order Details.
- Drafts can be saved before the customer, PO, location, or product is fully known.
- Added Continue Working to Customer Orders, showing the three most recently updated Draft/In Progress orders.
- Added Resume routing: unfinished detail-only drafts reopen in Order Details; active orders with tasks reopen in Order Workspace.
- Added currentStep and updatedAt fields for reliable continuation.

CHANGED
- Save Order is now Save & Continue and opens Order Workspace after saving.
- Opening an order workspace records the current step and safely moves Draft to In Progress.
- Completed workspace orders record currentStep as Completed.
