Date: 2026-09-02

PRODUCT LINK RECOVERY
- Detects inventory records whose Product Master ID is missing on the current device.
- Fetches the authoritative record-level Product Master and Inventory sets from Cloud Master.
- Requires the cloud sets to pass referential-integrity checks before any local repair.
- Restores only exact missing Product IDs; no fuzzy product-name guessing is used.

INVENTORY SAFETY
- Saves a local recovery point before repairing Product Master links.
- Does not change inventory quantities, locations, units, POs, lifecycle fields or history.
- Verifies the complete Inventory dataset is byte-for-byte unchanged after repair.
- Rolls Product Master back immediately if the inventory immutability check fails.

ONGOING PROTECTION
- Rechecks product/inventory links on boot, focus, page restore and while the app is open.
- Shows a clear recovery status above Inventory.
- Keeps unresolved records visible and unchanged instead of guessing or deleting them.
