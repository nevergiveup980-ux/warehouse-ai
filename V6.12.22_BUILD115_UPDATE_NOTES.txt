RUNLU Warehouse OS V6.12.22 Build115
Product Pricing Foundation
Date: 2026-09-04

WHAT CHANGED
- Adds a new Pricing & Cost section to Product Master.
- Stores supplier, purchase unit, sell unit and conversion factor with the product.
- Stores Standard Cost, Current / Last Supplier Cost and Standard Sell Price in CAD.
- Stores Price Source and an automatic pricing-updated timestamp.
- Uses the existing runlu_product_master_v21 product record, so the change is backward compatible with existing products.
- Existing products can be edited and priced without recreating inventory records.

DATA SAFETY
- No inventory quantity or location is changed by Build115.
- No carpet roll, PO, operation, transfer, receiving or history record is rewritten by Build115.
- Pricing data follows the existing Product Master local/cloud save path.
- If cloud pricing verification fails, the app keeps the local product data and warns the user.

FIELD ALIGNMENT
Build115 intentionally uses the same pricing vocabulary already established in Flooring OS pricing work: supplier, purchaseUnit, sellUnit, conversionFactor, standardCost, currentCost, retailPrice and priceSource. This avoids creating a second incompatible pricing model.

NEXT SAFE EXTENSIONS
Inventory value, gross-margin calculations, PO cost capture, price history and role-based cost visibility can now be built on top of this foundation without changing the inventory identity model.
